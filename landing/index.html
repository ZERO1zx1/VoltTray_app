Built like a native system utility

No vendor bloatware suites, no cloud accounts, and no risky device writes.

01 / BACKGROUND ALERTS

Hysteresis low-battery alerts

Alerts run directly in the Rust tray daemon—even while the popup is closed—using a +5% hysteresis band so notifications never spam at the threshold.

02 / STRICT READ-ONLY

Zero firmware or LED writes

VoltTray never alters DPI, RGB lighting, volume, or charge limits. Linux udev rules are strictly scoped to vendor HID telemetry interfaces—never keyboard input nodes.

03 / LOCAL-FIRST

Offline & zero telemetry

Only your four preferences (language, alerts, threshold, launch at login) are saved locally in state.json. Zero analytics SDKs, zero outbound requests.

Frequently asked questions

Why does my wired keyboard show “—” instead of 0%?

Wired devices without a battery do not report telemetry. VoltTray explicitly marks them as connected with no battery reading rather than faking 0% or triggering false alerts.

Can VoltTray set an 80% laptop charge limit or change RGB?

No. VoltTray is strictly read-only by design. Charge thresholds and LED settings remain under your OS or manufacturer firmware controls.
